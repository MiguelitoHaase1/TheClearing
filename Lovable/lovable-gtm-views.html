Enterprise Funnel

The GTM data does not describe a top-down enterprise sale. It describes a bottom-up pull that earns executive permission later.

Stage 1

Grow PQAs

Find companies with organic Lovable usage. Marketing and mid-size success stories create the initial product-qualified account signal.

Stage 2

Find PQLs

Convert team leads with budget. They need to see Lovable as more than a gimmick and be willing to sponsor bottom-up adoption.

Stage 3

Three-team pilot

Hyper-care pilots driven by team leads, not corporate theatre. The goal is beautiful stories and visible internal momentum.

Stage 4

CEO dogfooding

Use internal AI events and team proof to pull CEO, CEO-1, or CEO-2 into hands-on usage with an EA or PA.

Stage 5

Killer pitch

Bring Lovable, agencies, architecture, and security answers into a CEO/CTO conversation that moves shadow IT into the open.

Stage 6

Data project

FDEs and agencies build the governed data layer that lets the internal-tool motion scale safely across the enterprise.

Denmark Motion

Denmark is a high-trust, low-tolerance-for-sales market. The GTM motion has to feel like usefulness before it feels like selling.

Phase Move Why It Fits Denmark Output
Months 1-3 Seed champions at Danish tech companies, build live, use warm Nordic intros. Danish buyers distrust volume outreach but respond to credible people and visible craft. 10-15 champions, first local builds, public proof.
Months 3-6 Track product-qualified accounts and leads, then offer workspace, support, and structure. Consensus cultures need bottom-up evidence before top-down permission. Team pilots, PQL conversations, Danish success stories.
Months 6-12 Run community, land a lighthouse account, add trusted partners where they increase reach. Danish enterprise buyers look sideways at peers before they move. Reference account, repeatable motion, country playbook.

Strategic Tensions

These are not flaws in the work. They are the decisions the GTM system must make explicit.

Enterprise now vs. production later

The story wants enterprise urgency, while the roadmap still needs branching, access controls, audit trails, and security maturity.

Internal-only vs. load-bearing internal systems

Everyone says internal apps only, but a Slack replacement shows that internal can still be mission-critical.

Direct learning vs. consultancy leverage

Direct FDE work creates learning depth. Partners create reach. Denmark needs a clear rule for when to use each.

Learning lab vs. revenue market

The files describe Denmark as both a low-risk learning market and a meaningful TAM. The operating metric should choose which wins in months 1-3.

Domain Kits vs. Data Proxy

The recommended path is not either/or: sell department kits first, then let cross-department demand pull the proxy into existence.
